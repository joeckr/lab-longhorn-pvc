NOTE (Restricted PSS Workloads):
Application namespaces remain under `restricted` Pod Security Standards.
When mounting Longhorn PVCs in non-root pods (`runAsNonRoot: true`), set `spec.securityContext.fsGroup`
(e.g., `fsGroup: 1031` and `fsGroupChangePolicy: OnRootMismatch`) so kubelet grants group write permissions
on newly formatted ext4/xfs volumes.
